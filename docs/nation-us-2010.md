## Status and time boundary

This is a broad evidence profile for the [six-nation comparison]({{ '/nation-research/' | relative_url }}), researched on **25 September 2026**. Its institutional opening is **1 January 2010**, an editorial comparison convention, not a selected gameplay start date. No sector, first playable nation, calibrated baseline or runnable model is selected here. Proposed actor motives and consequences below are design hypotheses, not measured responses.

The player would lead the federal executive, beginning during Barack Obama's presidency. A useful opening briefing is: employment and housing remain under severe strain; financial support has created public stakes in particular companies; Congress controls new fiscal authority; states deliver important services; and the executive cannot order the Federal Reserve to lower rates. November 2009 unemployment was reported as 10.0% before the opening date, while the federal funds target was already 0–0.25%. [US03](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/empsit_12042009.htm), [US04](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/monetary20091216a.htm)

**Later developments must stay later.** The Affordable Care Act was enacted on 23 March 2010; Dodd–Frank on 21 July 2010. Neither supplies opening powers. Their provisions also have different implementation dates. The November midterm election belongs after the opening; its outcome must not be silently installed in January or guaranteed in a counterfactual campaign. A November election does not itself reset all congressional terms that day. [US02a](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/PLAW-111publ148/html/PLAW-111publ148.htm), [US02b](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/PLAW-111publ203/html/PLAW-111publ203.htm), [US01b](https://www.archives.gov/founding-docs/amendments-11-27)

Carbon-emissions and climate-change challenges are excluded. Historical publications may discuss them; those passages are not imported as game objectives, events, policies or coefficients. This boundary makes the game a selective economic model, not a complete historical reconstruction.

## Authority and practical limits

Congress comprises the House and Senate; federal legislation generally needs both chambers and presidential approval, subject to the veto-override route. Revenue bills originate in the House. Federal taxation, borrowing and commerce powers do not make the president a national production manager. The executive implements law and proposes changes; appointments do not erase an office's legal responsibilities. [US01a](https://www.archives.gov/founding-docs/constitution-transcript)

| Decision | Executive route | Other authority and binding limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Taxes and spending | Propose a bill and administer authorized programs | Congress legislates and appropriates; authorization, cash and delivery are separate. State/local budgets are separate accounts. US01a, US05 |
| Ownership or financial support | Use a specific existing investment authority, negotiate, or seek legislation | Property rights and compensation matter; a public equity stake has specified governance rights, not unlimited command. US01c, US06 |
| Competition and sector regulation | Set lawful administrative priorities; propose institutional changes | Relevant agencies apply their statutes and procedures. Competition enforcement is not a general presidential power to fix prices. US07 |
| Central banking and bank supervision | Coordinate information or seek legislation; use lawful appointments | FOMC monetary decisions are distinct from Treasury actions and prudential supervision. The pre-Dodd–Frank supervisory structure applies. US04, US08, US09 |
| Trade | Negotiate through the executive and administer delegated instruments | Congressional commerce powers, agreements and partner decisions constrain the route. NAFTA's US–Canada–Mexico institutions existed at opening. US01a, US10 |

**Budget fallback:** a continuing resolution is legislation that provides temporary funding, not an automatic rollover chosen by the president. GAO's September 2009 study documents restrictions on new starts, shortened commitments and extra administrative work under these resolutions. A funding lapse generally restricts agency operations under the Antideficiency Act; the future model must identify surviving appropriations and exceptions for the actual program. It must not shut every benefit and service off with one switch. This is documented implementation friction, not a guessed national “efficiency” score. [US05](https://www.gao.gov/assets/a295975.html)

**Legislative bargaining:** the Senate's historical account records the 1975 adoption of three-fifths cloture. This is a route for ending debate, distinct from the final passage vote; it is not a requirement that every bill receive sixty votes. Procedural exceptions and a dated January 2010 membership/vacancy record are still needed before assigning voting blocs. Party labels alone cannot supply guaranteed votes. [US11](https://www.senate.gov/about/powers-procedures/filibusters-cloture/overview.htm)

**Courts and regions:** judicial review can invalidate unconstitutional law; the executive does not receive immunity from review because a project is economically desirable. The Fifth Amendment supplies due-process and takings constraints; the Tenth reserves powers outside federal delegation. Selected policy cases still need period-specific standing, remedies and federal/state competence research. Public provision should therefore expose the actual implementing state, district or agency instead of treating all local authorities as executive departments. [US12](https://www.uscourts.gov/educational-resources/educational-activities/marbury-v-madison), [US01c](https://www.archives.gov/founding-docs/bill-of-rights-transcript)

**Succession:** the presidency has a four-year term; House terms are two years and Senate terms six years with staggered elections. Presidential removal is an impeachment-and-conviction route, not a parliamentary confidence vote. The Twenty-Fifth Amendment governs presidential replacement/incapacity, while the Twenty-Second limits election to the presidency. Outstanding contracts and debt should persist across leadership changes; that persistence is a proposed game rule. [US01a](https://www.archives.gov/founding-docs/constitution-transcript), [US01b](https://www.archives.gov/founding-docs/amendments-11-27)

## Ownership, services and households

The opening economy cannot be represented by “private ownership” alone. Treasury announced additional GMAC capital and an increase in common-equity ownership to roughly 56% on 30 December 2009, with rights ultimately to appoint four of nine directors. That announcement distinguishes share ownership, board appointments and management. General Motors and Chrysler had also required emergency support during the crisis. These are dated examples of intervention in a market economy; they do not establish a general state allocation system. [US06](https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/tg501), [US13a](https://obamawhitehouse.archives.gov/sites/default/files/microsites/economic-report-president-chapter-2r2.pdf)

The FTC's FY2009 report documents merger and conduct enforcement. Ownership and competition are therefore separate dimensions: a private merger can face review, and support to one producer does not establish competitive conditions across its industry. No concentration ratio or corporate influence score is supplied here. Those require a named product market and period. [US07](https://www.ftc.gov/sites/default/files/documents/reports_annual/performance-and-accountability-report/2009parreport.pdf)

**Healthcare:** the pre-ACA arrangement combined employer-linked and individual insurance with public programs. Medicare and Medicaid are different institutions, not one universal service. Medicaid's federal/state financing connected falling state revenue with rising eligibility pressure during the downturn. The February 2010 presidential report describes this existing arrangement and the Recovery Act's temporary matching support, while advocating further reform. Its advocacy is not independent proof that the proposed reforms would succeed. A player proposal must distinguish coverage eligibility, financing and the capacity of providers to deliver care. [US13b](https://obamawhitehouse.archives.gov/sites/default/files/microsites/economic-report-president-chapter-7r2.pdf)

**Education and work:** the period report describes public, nonprofit and for-profit postsecondary institutions, tuition and fees, federal financial assistance, and community colleges' training role. Expanding assistance and expanding teaching capacity are different interventions. A grant can reduce a household's financing barrier without supplying an instructor or matching qualifications to vacancies. The latter sentence is a proposed mechanism to investigate, not a calibrated effect. Precise state/local school-finance shares remain outside this extraction. [US13c](https://obamawhitehouse.archives.gov/sites/default/files/microsites/economic-report-president-chapter-8r2.pdf)

**Housing and income protection:** HUD's voucher reporting system collected information from housing authorities, demonstrating a delivery layer beyond the federal executive. The source inventory identifies these administrative records but does not certify a January 2010 voucher count. Mortgage distress and refinancing obstacles are separate from rental assistance. Social Security's 2010 statistical supplement distinguishes old-age, survivors and disability insurance, means-tested Supplemental Security Income, and unemployment insurance. A single “welfare generosity” slider would hide different eligibility, financing and delivery routes. [US14](https://catalog.data.gov/dataset/housing-choice-voucher-program-data), [US15](https://www.ssa.gov/policy/docs/statcomps/supplement/2010/)

**Household differences:** November 2009's release reports 5.9 million long-term unemployed people and differing unemployment rates across age and demographic groups. These observations support separating jobless households from secure earners, not assigning immutable traits. The later Census report finds different regional income changes during 2010; those outcomes are comparison evidence, unavailable at opening. Age, household composition, disability, migration status, wealth and debt are separate candidate dimensions. Their joint distribution has not been reconstructed. [US03](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/empsit_12042009.htm), [US16](https://www.census.gov/newsroom/releases/archives/income_wealth/cb11-157.html)

## Production, infrastructure and external dependencies

The 2010 presidential report identifies trade in services and agricultural products alongside manufacturing; its product examples include aircraft and integrated circuits on the export side and clothing, furniture and consumer electronics on the import side. These examples establish differentiated exposure, not sector rankings or a national self-sufficiency score. The crisis chapter also connects business activity to commercial paper, bank loans and credit-financed purchases. A producer's financing channel should therefore depend on its type and circumstances. [US13d](https://obamawhitehouse.archives.gov/sites/default/files/microsites/economic-report-president-chapter-10r2.pdf), [US13a](https://obamawhitehouse.archives.gov/sites/default/files/microsites/economic-report-president-chapter-2r2.pdf)

An EIA review published in April 2011 reports that half of 2010 liquid-fuel net imports came from the Americas. This is a retrospective supply observation, not an opening forecast, and its definition includes more than crude oil. Fuel availability, refineries, transport and delivered prices are relevant research channels. No disruption probability or substitution capacity is inferred from the import share. [US17](https://www.eia.gov/todayinenergy/detail.php?id=970)

The October 2009 NAFTA commission statement shows partner coordination on rules of origin, trade facilitation and small-firm access. A domestic announcement cannot by itself change a partner's obligations. Product-specific tariff schedules, WTO commitments, procurement coverage and trade-remedy procedures remain required before offering a particular trade action. Infrastructure ownership and procurement authority likewise need project-level research; this broad profile does not certify a national construction pipeline. [US10](https://ustr.gov/about-us/policy-offices/press-office/press-releases/2009/october/joint-statement-2009-nafta-commission-meeting)

## Money, banking and public finances

The United States had its own dollar monetary system and independent Federal Reserve. The December 2009 FOMC statement maintained a 0–0.25% funds-rate target and described planned agency-debt and mortgage-backed-security purchases. A 2007 governor's speech explains the existing employment and price-stability mandate. Do not import the later formal 2% inflation framework into the opening. The near-zero policy rate did not mean every household or business could borrow at that rate. [US04](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/monetary20091216a.htm), [US09](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/speech/mishkin20070410a.htm), [US13a](https://obamawhitehouse.archives.gov/sites/default/files/microsites/economic-report-president-chapter-2r2.pdf)

The Federal Reserve's 2009 supervision report identifies its responsibility for bank holding companies and state member banks, alongside coordination with other supervisors. It also documents deteriorating loans and enforcement action. Monetary accommodation, capital support, supervision and a bank's lending decision must remain separate. An institution can have funding yet face losses, weak collateral or poor borrower demand. A separate Federal Reserve study names JPMorgan Chase and Bank of America among the large holding companies assessed in 2009 and supplies commercial-bank balance-sheet categories. These are candidate historical actors, not fictional behavior assignments. [US19](https://www.federalreserve.gov/pubs/bulletin/2010/articles/profit/) The exact OCC/FDIC/OTS/state-charter allocation and individual private-bank balance sheets remain dependent research; do not install post-Dodd–Frank agencies or powers at opening. [US08](https://www.federalreserve.gov/boarddocs/rptcongress/annual09/sec2/c1.htm)

Treasury's FY2009 financial report distinguishes debt held by the public from intragovernmental holdings and records bills, notes, bonds and inflation-protected securities. It also records the statutory debt ceiling, distinct from appropriations. Published in February 2010, it can establish earlier structures but was not itself an opening information release. Dollar amounts, original-term categories and average rates do not supply a remaining-maturity schedule for 1 January. The first model would need issue-level or maturity-bucket evidence, cash timing, coupon treatment and an explicit treatment of indexed principal. [US18](https://fiscal.treasury.gov/system/files/files/reports-statements/financial-report/09frusg.pdf)

**Design implication:** separate new borrowing costs, inherited debt service, bank-credit terms and monetary decisions. No debt/GDP threshold, interest surcharge, exchange-rate response or fiscal multiplier is established by this profile. A dollar financing model still needs lawful borrowing authority, investor behavior and real delivery constraints; it cannot treat issuance as free productive capacity.

## Candidate policy families

These are research possibilities, not selected mechanics. Motives and consequences in the final column are explicit hypotheses for later evidence and testing.

| Family | Possible route and relevant institutions | Motives, constraints and possible consequences |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Employment and income support | Seek congressional tax/benefit legislation or funded grants; identify federal and state administrators. US01a, US15 | Jobless households may value near-term support; lawmakers may contest targeting and duration. Spending could stabilize consumption, while expiring support could create a later income drop. Neither magnitude is established. |
| Health coverage and state finances | Amend relevant legislation or use an existing, verified delegation; negotiate financing and implementation with states. US13b | States may seek federal matching funds; providers need payment and capacity. Broader eligibility could improve access while increasing administrative and budget needs. ACA passage is an available historical comparison, not guaranteed gameplay. |
| Housing and credit restructuring | Identify Treasury/HUD authority, appropriation, lender contracts and implementing housing authorities. US14, US18 | Borrowers may seek affordable payments; lenders and investors may dispute loss allocation. Restructuring could avert some distress but cannot guarantee recovery of employment or property values. |
| Competition, ownership and business finance | Apply the relevant regulator's mandate, negotiate an investment, or seek legislation; distinguish shareholder and supervisory roles. US06–US08 | Managers may seek capital and discretion; workers may seek continuity; taxpayers may demand an exit. Support could preserve operations while exposing public money to losses and changing competitive conditions. |
| Education, infrastructure and trade access | Fund a named program through Congress and the relevant implementer; negotiate international changes where needed. US10, US13c–US13d | Providers and exporters may seek capacity and market access. Benefits could arrive after financing commitments, with construction, staffing or partner negotiations causing delay. |

## Gaps that block a playable baseline

1. **Legislative and litigation encounters:** establish January membership/vacancies, procedural routes for the selected bill, relevant statutory delegation and period case law. These block vote counts, legal challenges and success/failure branches.
2. **Federal/state service delivery:** choose a bounded geographic representation and retrieve program eligibility, grants, matching rules, school/housing authority powers and usable pre-opening administrative extracts. These block a common “government can build/provide” action.
3. **Finance transmission:** reconcile a dated Treasury stock and remaining maturities; retrieve private-bank and household debt categories, fixed/variable contracts and supervisory assignment. These block debt-service repricing and lending responses.
4. **Production and distribution:** obtain BEA sector accounts, selected infrastructure ownership/capacity, household income/assets/debts, age and migration distributions, and product-level trade inputs. These block resource balances and credible group effects; national averages cannot fill them.
5. **Policy comparison:** select a first nation and policy family, then specify at least two alternatives plus continuation, implementation timing, financing counterparts and evidence-backed behavioral ranges. This profile certifies no policy multiplier, winning strategy or simulated result.

**Preferred next step:** compare this profile with the other five before selecting the first playable nation. **Alternative:** audit the US authority and delivery route for one candidate policy if the comparison identifies it as the strongest starting point.
