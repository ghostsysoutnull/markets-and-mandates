## Status and time boundary

This broad profile supports the [six-nation comparison]({{ '/nation-research/' | relative_url }}). Research date: **25 September 2026**. The institutional opening is **1 January 2010**, an editorial convention rather than a selected gameplay start. No first playable nation or policy family is selected; no calibrated baseline or playable model is supplied. Actor motives and consequences proposed below are hypotheses requiring further evidence.

Opening officeholders were **Hu Jintao**, CPC general secretary and state president, and **Wen Jiabao**, premier. A contemporaneous Foreign Ministry account identifies their separate offices. [CN17](https://www.fmprc.gov.cn/mfa_eng/gjhdq_665435/2675_665437/2701_663406/2703_663410/202406/t20240607_11407272.html)

The opening briefing would emphasize recovery following the external-demand shock, investment financed through several public and commercial channels, and the difficulty of extending services across localities. The accepted player role is executive leadership; it does not automatically include control of every Party organ or the NPC. The precise playable office and Party-coordination arrangements remain to specify. Substantial coordinating power would still require budgets, implementing organizations, viable projects and information about borrowers. National direction does not make every enterprise balance sheet interchangeable with the Treasury.

The scope concerns mainland economic institutions. Hong Kong and Macao require separate legal, currency and statistical treatment; a corporate example involving Hong Kong below establishes ownership, not mainland regulatory jurisdiction. Taiwan is not folded into the mainland model. Carbon-emissions and climate-change challenges are outside the game scope, even where historical sources discuss them.

**Historical boundary:** use the Constitution as amended in **2004**, the **2007** CPC Constitution and the **1994** Budget Law. Do not backdate later constitutional or financial-supervision changes. The June 2010 exchange-rate announcement, March budget and full-year statistics are later information. A reform announcement is not evidence that its promised coverage or service quality was already achieved.

## Authority, leadership and implementation

State and Party offices need separate representation. Under the period state Constitution, the NPC is the highest state-power organ; it selects the president and decides the premier on presidential nomination. The State Council administers government. The NPC Standing Committee supervises constitutional implementation. Courts exercise judicial power under law and are responsible to the relevant people's congresses; the text alone does not establish effective judicial independence. [CN01](https://lpr.adb.org/document/constitution-peoples-republic-china-2004-amendment)

The 2007 CPC Constitution makes the Central Committee's plenum the elector of the Politburo, its Standing Committee and the general secretary. Party groups in state bodies address major matters and cadre management. Consequently, legislative, administrative and Party channels cannot be modelled as independent competing executives, or reduced to personal presidential commands. This is an institutional interpretation; the precise informal bargaining and appointment networks remain unmeasured. [CN02](http://www.china.org.cn/english/congress/229722.htm)

| Decision | Formal route in the opening period | Practical distinction for a future model |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Taxes and expenditure | Government prepares and implements budgets; NPC approves the central budget, with corresponding local congress responsibilities. Collection must follow law or administrative regulations. CN03 | An approved national priority still needs a responsible spending body and local funding; no universal executive tax slider. |
| Public ownership and competition | Public ownership has a constitutional leading position alongside lawful private activity; the Anti-Monopoly Law supplies conduct and merger rules. CN01, CN04 | Shareholder direction, regulation and procurement are different powers; public ownership is not unlimited exemption from competition law. |
| Money and exchange policy | PBC operates under State Council leadership; major money-supply, rate and exchange decisions require State Council approval. CN05 | National coordination differs from local officials ordering a branch to fund projects. |
| Bank supervision | The State Council's banking regulator supervises banking institutions under the period supervision law. CN06 | In 2010 this is the CBRC framework; later consolidated supervisors must not appear at opening. |
| Trade and investment | Domestic measures operate within China's WTO commitments. CN07 | A national policy instruction cannot guarantee foreign demand, market access or foreign approval. |

**Budget fallback:** Article 44 of the 1994 Budget Law permits expenditure before budget approval using the corresponding previous-year period's expenditure as the basis; the approved budget then applies. Article 28 requires balanced local budgets and prohibits local bond issuance **except where laws or the State Council provide otherwise**. Treating this as either unlimited local borrowing or an absolute historical ban would be wrong. [CN03](http://www.npc.gov.cn/zgrdw/englishnpc/Law/2007-12/12/content_1383623.htm)

**Succession:** the period presidency had a five-year term and a two-consecutive-term limit; presidential vacancy passed to the vice-president. These rules do not automatically define the general secretary's tenure. Party congress and Central Committee processes require their own calendar. Leadership succession should not erase outstanding obligations; that persistence is a proposed game rule. [CN01](https://lpr.adb.org/document/constitution-peoples-republic-china-2004-amendment), [CN02](http://www.china.org.cn/english/congress/229722.htm)

**Observed central–local limits:** the World Bank's March 2010 assessment describes local investment companies borrowing for infrastructure, commonly supported by land-related revenue or collateral. Their liabilities could become public obligations through guarantees or operating subsidies. This is evidence of financing and information problems inside a strongly directed system. It does not supply a probability that a mayor disobeys, a project fails or a national rescue occurs. [CN08](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/226741468216275514/pdf/545050NEWS0ENG10Box349420B01PUBLIC1.pdf)

## Ownership, production and external connections

The constitutional ownership settlement combines state, collective and non-public activity. Urban land is state-owned; rural land is generally collectively owned, subject to the constitutional exceptions. Ownership of land, transferable use rights, ownership of a building and receipt of development proceeds must be separate concepts. [CN01](https://lpr.adb.org/document/constitution-peoples-republic-china-2004-amendment)

Competition law had been effective since August 2008. Article 7 protects lawful operations in specified state-controlled strategic industries while requiring supervision and protection of consumers; Article 8 prohibits administrative abuse restricting competition. A proposal to ease market entry should identify the actual licence, authority and affected market. The existence of this law is not evidence that all barriers were removed or enforcement was uniform. [CN04](http://www.npc.gov.cn/zgrdw/englishnpc/Law/2009-02/20/content_1471587.htm)

**Concrete enterprise:** Geely Automobile's 2009 report records its chairman and concert parties at the top of the ownership chart, subsidiaries with different ownership shares, manufacturing plants and overseas transmission operations acquired in June 2009. Management reported stronger domestic sales alongside weak exports. This illustrates private control, supplier dependence and external-market exposure without making one producer representative of China. Company explanations of performance remain management's account. [CN09](https://www.geelyauto.com.hk/core/files/financial/en/2009-02.pdf)

The WTO's May–June 2010 review describes the preceding external-demand collapse and investment-led response, and identifies continuing restrictions in services and investment. It also records that accession to the Government Procurement Agreement remained unfinished. Trade commitments, domestic procurement and foreign-investment access therefore need distinct policy routes. [CN07](https://www.wto.org/english/tratop_e/tpr_e/tp330_e.htm)

For a broad game, candidate production systems include agriculture and food distribution, manufacturing supply chains, construction, transport and commercial services. Each needs inputs, workers, customers and financing. The sector shares below establish composition, not a capacity map. Ports, roads, rail links, fuel deliveries and electricity reliability would be investigated as dependencies only where a chosen policy requires them. No electricity-first research direction follows from this profile.

## Households, services and regional differences

A national household average cannot represent local residents, migrants, farmers, salaried employees and retirees equally. The OECD's February 2010 survey explains that hukou distinguishes both registration location and agricultural/non-agricultural status. A person living in a city can lack local registration; provinces and cities differed in conversion rules. Residence, registration and access to benefits must therefore be separate fields. The survey also describes fragmented old-age arrangements and health financing. Its recommended reforms are analysis, not implemented national rules. [CN10](https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/publications/reports/2010/02/oecd-economic-surveys-china-2010_g1ghbd4a/eco_surveys-chn-2010-en.pdf)

**Healthcare:** the January 2009 State Council plan targeted broader basic coverage, an essential-medicines system, stronger grassroots facilities, public-health access and public-hospital pilots. Its three-year financing commitment was a plan, not money already spent or universal effective coverage. A future service model should distinguish insurance eligibility, reimbursement, affordable medicine and available clinical capacity. A subsidy cannot by itself supply a qualified practitioner. [CN11](https://chicago.china-consulate.gov.cn/eng/lmbf/ywzn/kj/200901/t20090122_5497157.htm)

**Education, housing and pensions:** the Ministry of Finance's March 2010 report describes 2009 support for compulsory education, vocational education, low-income housing and the start of rural pension pilots. It also reports central transfers and separate land-related government-fund accounts. These are distinct financing and eligibility arrangements, not one universal entitlement. The report's 2010 proposals must remain separate from its 2009 implementation account. [CN12](http://www.npc.gov.cn/zgrdw/englishnpc/Special_11_4/2010-03/19/content_1621705.htm)

**Proposed household interests:** a migrant family may prioritize school admission, stable rental housing and portable health protection; an existing urban resident may value service capacity and housing security; a farmer may value dependable income and land-use security; a retiree may prioritize benefit reliability. These are testable actor hypotheses, not fixed preferences assigned to every member of a group. Demographic change should enter through dated age and household distributions, which have not been extracted here. National birth, ageing or migration rules cannot be inferred from the registered-unemployment statistic.

## Banking, currency and public finance

The PBC law specifies currency-value stability in support of growth, State Council leadership and protection from local interference. It prohibits Treasury overdrafts and direct subscription for or underwriting of government bonds. Political coordination thus does not imply that every financing channel is legally available. The bank-supervision law separately covers prudential oversight and interventions. [CN05](https://haikou.pbc.gov.cn/eportal/fileDir/image_public/UserFiles/haikou/upload/File/%E4%B8%AD%E5%8D%8E%E4%BA%BA%E6%B0%91%E5%85%B1%E5%92%8C%E5%9B%BD%E4%B8%AD%E5%9B%BD%E4%BA%BA%E6%B0%91%E9%93%B6%E8%A1%8C%E6%B3%95.pdf), [CN06](http://www.npc.gov.cn/zgrdw/englishnpc/Law/2007-12/05/content_1381962.htm)

The PBC's **19 June 2010** statement describes the managed floating framework introduced in 2005 and the exchange rate's stability during the crisis, then announces further flexibility. That later decision is not an opening permission or an inevitable outcome. The exact January trading band, operational instruments and capital-account restrictions remain extraction tasks before implementing an exchange screen. [CN13](https://www.pbc.gov.cn/english/130721/2025080815063481973/index.html)

**Ownership examples:** ICBC Asia's 2009 report identifies its parent ICBC and documents the Ministry of Finance and Central Huijin each controlling at least one-third of ICBC voting power at year-end. This is evidence of public control through a listed banking group, not a mainland banking licence for its Hong Kong subsidiary. Minsheng's corporate history dates its establishment by predominantly non-state enterprises to 1996; its exact January 2010 shareholder and control structure still needs the period annual report. Neither example licenses a claim that every loan follows the same allocation rule. [CN14](https://www.icbc.com.mo/SiteCollectionDocuments/ICBC/Resources/ICBC/haiwai/Asia/download/EN/2014/100423_AnnualReport_Eng.pdf), [CN15](https://en.cmbc.com.cn/cmbc_en/cmbc_today/about_cmbc/introduction_to_cmbc/index.html)

Local general budgets, government-managed funds and investment-company borrowing must be recorded separately before consolidation. The World Bank distinguishes current revenue and transfers from land-related finance and lending. A company's debt is not automatically sovereign debt, but omitting contingent public support would conceal exposure. [CN08](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/226741468216275514/pdf/545050NEWS0ENG10Box349420B01PUBLIC1.pdf)

The fiscal report records Ministry of Finance issuance on behalf of local governments with State Council approval in 2009, illustrating the legal exception above. The central-government bond stock below excludes a complete local/platform liability map. Currency, coupon, maturity, creditor and guarantee data remain required; no interest-rate, rollover or rescue formula is calibrated. [CN12](http://www.npc.gov.cn/zgrdw/englishnpc/Special_11_4/2010-03/19/content_1621705.htm)

## Policy possibilities and actor motives

These are alternatives for later specification, not selected actions or promised effects. The legal anchors are CN03–CN08 and CN10–CN13; all behavioural consequences in this table are hypotheses.

| Candidate | Route and actors | Motives, limits and possible consequences |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Improve local service funding | Central budget/transfers, local budgets and implementing schools or clinics | Households may seek access; local officials may resist an unfunded mandate. Predictable grants could support staffing, while poorly specified eligibility could leave migrants uncovered. |
| Review infrastructure lending and guarantees | PBC/State Council monetary route, CBRC oversight, bank and platform accounts | Banks may protect repayment; local authorities may protect unfinished projects and employment. Tighter screening could reduce future losses while interrupting viable construction. Existing contracts need a treatment rule. |
| Broaden affordable housing provision | Budget authority, local land-use and housing bodies, actual providers | Renters may seek lower costs; developers and local finances may depend on transactions. More units could improve access, but locations, infrastructure, allocation and maintenance determine usefulness. |
| Reduce barriers to business entry | Identify sector rules and use lawful regulatory or competition powers | Entrants may want access; incumbents may defend revenues or service obligations. Entry could improve choice, but a licence change alone does not create finance, skilled staff or distribution. |
| Adjust exchange-policy implementation | State Council approval and PBC operations within the period framework | Import users and exporters may have different exposures; borrowers may care about foreign-currency liabilities. Price and investment effects require contracts, pass-through estimates and external responses. |

A future player briefing should explain which actor can decide, who pays and who implements each option. Even where Party coordination aligns formal institutions, capacity, information and competing organizational interests can remain binding. Those frictions should be demonstrated with a selected delivery chain rather than invented as a national personality score.

## Gaps before a playable model

| Dependent feature | Specific evidence still required |
| --- | --- |
| Leadership and implementation friction | A full dated committee roster, the precise playable executive office and Party-coordination boundaries, and an actual central-to-local implementation case; period court remedies and administrative appeal practice. Formal texts cannot calibrate compliance or judicial delay. |
| Service eligibility and mobility | One locality's 2009–10 school, health, pension and housing eligibility, transfer rules, recurrent budgets and delivery capacity. National plan targets cannot supply household entitlements. |
| Public debt and bank responses | Central maturity/currency schedule; selected local/platform loan and guarantee registers; period bank ownership, prudential rules and loss treatment. No universal bailout assumption. |
| Prices and external transactions | January 2010 exchange operations, capital restrictions, sector import inputs and contracts; dated consumer basket and household exposures. No guessed exchange pass-through. |
| Production and demographics | Comparable regional accounts, age/household distributions, firm capacities and ownership with denominator definitions; validate census revisions against the communiqué. |

An alternative is a short cross-nation evidence-gap review before selection. Neither option authorizes coding or assumes an electricity focus.
